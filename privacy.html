gigly Privacy Policy

Effective October 6, 2026. Updated October 15, 2026

This explains what gigly collects, why, and the choices you have. We don't sell your data and we don't show ads.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address and password (stored securely by our authentication provider, never in readable form). When you sign up we also record that you confirmed you're 18 or older and agreed to our terms, and when.
  • Profile: what you choose to add, including username, roles, city, tagline, bio, genres, influences, who you're looking for, and availability. Your city is whatever you type. We don't track your GPS location.
  • Pictures: your profile photo, pictures you add to your influences, and cover art you add to demos. You choose each one. We don't access the rest of your photo library or your camera.
  • Audio: demos and layers you record or upload.
  • Messages and activity: messages with people you're connected with, reactions and replies, connection requests, follows, blocks, and whether a message was delivered or read.
  • Group chats: the groups you create or join (name and who is in them), the messages you send in them, invites, and short notices such as "someone joined the group" or "someone left".
  • Your look: if you choose a color theme, dark mode, or greyscale, we save that choice on your account so it follows you to a new phone.
  • Problem details: if the app hits a problem, it keeps a short list of recent errors on your phone (what failed and when, never your messages or audio). It is only shared if you choose to include it in an email to us.
  • Reports: reports you send, including the reason and any details you add.
  • Notifications: if you allow them, an identifier for your phone (a push token) so we can alert you about messages, group messages, connection requests, layers on your ideas, and reactions to your messages. Notifications show who it's from, never the text of a message.
  • Device permissions: the microphone, only when you record; your files and pictures, only when you choose one to upload; and notifications, only if you allow them.

How we use it

To run gigly: sign you in, show your profile, play your demos, deliver messages and notifications, suggest musicians to you, and keep the community safe, including reviewing reports and stopping abuse. We don't use your information for advertising.

Who can see what

  • Signed-in users can see your profile, pictures, and demos, unless you turn on Hide my profile or block them.
  • With Hide my profile on, you're hidden from Discover and Home, and people who aren't connected with you can't load your pictures. People you're connected with can still see you.
  • Only you and the person you're chatting with can read your messages. In a group chat, every member can read the group's messages.
  • People in the same group can see each other's username and profile picture, even if they aren't otherwise connected. If you block someone, you stop seeing their group messages, but they stay in the group and still see yours.
  • Messages aren't end-to-end encrypted, so we can technically access them. We only look when needed to handle a report, keep gigly safe, or follow the law.
  • Reports are private. The person you report isn't told who reported them. We may view reported content, including pictures, to review a report.

Who we share it with

Our hosting, database, authentication, and file-storage provider, Supabase, processes data for us so the app works, including storing your audio and pictures. Notifications are delivered through Expo's notification service and Google's Firebase Cloud Messaging, which receive your notification address and the short alert text. If you use gigly in a web browser, the website is served by Cloudflare, which handles the web traffic (such as your IP address) needed to deliver the page. In a browser, gigly keeps your sign-in and settings in that browser's own storage. When we send email, we'll use an email delivery provider. We may share information if the law requires it, or to protect someone's safety. We don't sell personal information.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is active. When you remove a picture or delete a demo, we delete the file. If you delete your account, we delete your profile, pictures, demos, messages, group messages and memberships, and notification address right away. A group with nobody left in it is deleted too. Copies in routine backups expire on a regular schedule. We may keep limited records, such as reports, where needed for safety or legal reasons.

Your choices

  • Edit or remove profile information, pictures, demos, and layers in the app.
  • Hide your profile, or block and report anyone, in the app.
  • Leave any group chat whenever you like. Nobody can add you to a group: you only join if you accept an invite.
  • Change or reset your look any time under Settings, then Appearance.
  • Turn notifications off any time in your phone's settings.
  • Delete your account in the app under Settings, then Privacy and safety.
  • Ask us for a copy of your data, a correction, or deletion of your account by emailing cristofrbarrett@gmail.com. We'll respond within 30 days.

Security

We use access controls so users can only read what they're allowed to. No service is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

Age limit

gigly is for people 18 and older. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18, and we'll delete it if we learn we have.

Changes

If we change this policy in a meaningful way, we'll tell you in the app before it takes effect.
